# Code of Conduct

Scratch is built by a small team and anyone who wants to help. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented, to have a good experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Keep discussions focused on making Scratch better.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring requests to stop

## Enforcement

The maintainers (the developers listed in the README) may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block people from the project, temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, email one of the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.
